Entry for [One Line, One Laugh: The Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-myronera-one-line-one-laugh-the-office-joke-open-fe8204d4) — a single one-line joke about office life, at most 200 characters. A model judge compares entries two at a time until the funniest line is left standing.

## The joke

> Our manager moved the 9 a.m. stand-up to 8:45 so we'd 'start sharp' — now fifteen of us stand in a circle at dawn, quietly resenting a man named Greg.

150 characters.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. The gate requires UTF-8 text of 1 to 200 characters, not only white space; the program also checks that itself before writing.
